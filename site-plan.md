## Visual Direction

- Palette: warm white `#F7F5EF` for the page background, charcoal `#202A33` for text, leafy green `#3D6B5A` for primary accents, and soft coral `#E27D60` for secondary accents.
- Fonts: Fraunces for headings and Source Sans 3 for body text, with Georgia and sans-serif fallbacks.
- The wireframes describe content order only; styling will be added in a later step.
